# Brazilian professional & small-business niches: painful workflows, incumbents, arbitrage gaps and validated product opportunities (research notes, compiled 2026-10-12)

*Method: about 60 web searches, direct reads of about 45 vendor and official pages on 2026-10-12, and a Google Autocomplete (pt-BR, gl=br) scan of 80 seed terms on 2026-10-12. The shared web-search budget ran out partway through, so several planned checks are listed under Gaps. Prices are vendor-published list prices, so treat them as self-reported, and customer counts are vendor claims unless noted otherwise. "Accessed 2026-10-12" means the live page was read on that date. The brief's implied FX rate (R$700 ≈ US$130, about R$5.4/US$) is used, so US$5k/month ≈ R$27k/month.*

## 1. By professional group: manual workflows, incumbents and prices, complaints, headcounts, and saturation (core practice management vs. narrow sub-workflows)

### Takeaway
For every group studied, core practice management is saturated or dominated by incumbents with large self-reported user bases: iClinic (50k+ doctors), Astrea (120k+ users), ADVBOX (5k+ offices) and SimplesVet. The gaps that remain are narrow, document-heavy, compliance-triggered sub-workflows that are still done in Word, Excel or WhatsApp. The least-served are engineering-grade field reports (vistoria cautelar de vizinhança, inspeção predial/autovistoria, vistoria de recebimento de imóvel), PTAM appraisals by brokers, and service certificates (dedetização, limpeza de caixa d'água). Several sub-workflows that looked open in 2024 filled up with small, often AI-built entrants in 2025–2026: SST/NR-1 psychosocial, perito management, síndico tools, landlord self-management, rental inspections and PMOC.

#### 1.4 Lawyers (DJEN, Domicílio Judicial Eletrônico and deadlines)

## 2. Geographic arbitrage: US/global tools with proven revenue vs. Brazilian equivalents (Pix, NF-e/NFS-e, WhatsApp, LGPD, CLT, Portuguese)

### Takeaway
Most US small-business categories already have Brazilian equivalents: practice management, field service, condo and STR management, landlord tools and accounting portals. The clearest under-served transfer is Spectora-style engineering-grade inspection reporting, combined with CompanyCam-style photo documentation, for Brazilian engineers and architects. Today that work is done in Word or Excel, and the existing Brazilian apps target rental inspections. A HoneyBook-style flow (proposal → contract → Pix installments) is thinly covered in Brazil, but local willingness to pay is unproven.

### Inferences
- **The best transfer is a Spectora-for-Brazilian-engineering report generator.** US proof exists (a ~US$5.9M-revenue niche tool at about US$100/mo per seat). - **Jobber-style generic field service is already covered in Brazil.** Arbitrage there only works in regulated verticals where a legally required document is the core output, such as pest-control certificates or water-tank cleaning certificates.

### Inferences
- **The comfortable price band** for a narrow professional tool sold online is about R$49–149/month (R$490–1,490/year). Per-document pricing of about R$20–100 works when the document is billed to a client.
- **Reaching about R$27k/month (US$5k)** at R$99–149 average revenue per user needs roughly 180–275 paying users, which is plausible in pools of tens of thousands of professionals. Per-report models need about 400–900 paid reports a month.
- **What a newcomer should offer:** transparent flat pricing (no percentage fees, no add-on creep), a free tier or free templates for SEO, and an annual Pix prepay option. Quote-only competitors leave room for self-serve pricing.

## 4. Objective synthesis: candidate opportunities (full records, 1–5 scores, exclusions), top 2, and traps

### Takeaway
Seven candidates survive the constraints. Two stand out. The first is an **engineering-grade field inspection report generator** (vistoria cautelar de vizinhança, vistoria de recebimento de imóvel novo, inspeção predial/autovistoria) for engineers and architects. The second is a **deterministic PTAM generator** for CRECI brokers. The best alternate if PTAM's legal or data risks prove unacceptable is a **pest-control and water-tank-cleaning certificate and recall tool**. Many popular-sounding ideas are traps: DJEN monitors, landlord apps, rental vistorias, PMOC, NR-1 psychosocial questionnaires, Airbnb tools, Receita Saúde helpers and practice management for any health or legal profession.

#### Candidate records

**A. Engineering field-inspection report generator ("Spectora for Brazilian engineering")**
- **Product (one line):** an offline-first web app for phones (PWA) in which an engineer or architect captures photos tagged by property, room, building element and anomaly, rates severity, and exports a standardized, branded DOCX or PDF laudo with numbered photo grids, an ART/RRT field and a signature block, plus a share link for the client.
  - It ships with three templates: vistoria cautelar de vizinhança (batch: one job produces N neighbour reports), vistoria de recebimento de imóvel novo, and inspeção predial/autovistoria.
- **Buyer and size:** civil engineers, architects and small engineering firms that sell vistorias and laudos. The pool is about 1.2M Confea registrants (all engineering, agronomy and geoscience) plus more than 200k architects. The share that sells these reports is unknown; even 1% would be about 14k professionals.
- **Income:** 68% of Confea professionals' families earn more than 5 minimum wages.
- **Pain:** reports with dozens to hundreds of photos are laid out by hand in Word or Excel. This shows in queries such as "relatório fotográfico word download", "…de obra excel grátis", "laudo de vistoria cautelar de vizinhança doc/pdf/modelo" and "laudo de inspeção predial word". Layout hours are expensive against a R$625/h benchmark.
- **Competitors:**
  - Howse: R$56–70/mo, 20 vistorias, AI conclusions; no customer count.
  - Onlaine and Inspeção Pro: prices not public.
  - Hauseful Checklist: no price.
  - Afirma Vistoria Fácil: for construtoras, R$1,500 sign-up fee.
  - Rental apps (Vistortec, Devolus) target real-estate agencies.
  - No revenue figures are known; all claims are self-reported.
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Most tools are built for rental inspections or as generic checklists.
  - AI-centred pitches.
  - Hidden prices (Onlaine, Inspeção Pro).
  - It is unverified whether any tool does multi-property cautelar batches or NBR 16747-style risk prioritization out of the box.
- **Demand signals:** 9–10 autocomplete suggestions on six report-related seeds; strong "modelo/word/pdf" intent. No absolute volumes.
- **Acquisition without contacts:**
  - Free DOCX template pages matching each autocomplete query, each with a "generate this report automatically" upsell.
  - YouTube and Instagram walkthroughs.
  - Engineering Facebook and LinkedIn groups.
  - A Google Ads test of about R$300–400 on long-tail template queries.
- **Build complexity:** medium; an MVP in about 2–4 weeks with Claude Code. It needs offline photo storage, compression, a DOCX/PDF layout engine and three templates, and payments through a gateway with Pix and card.
- **Time to first revenue:** about 4–8 weeks. Paid traffic can bring the first trial users earlier than SEO.
- **Main risks:**
  - A template culture may mean low willingness to pay among individuals.
  - Howse or the rental apps could copy the features.
  - Photo storage costs.
  - The report formats must satisfy ABNT/IBAPE expectations; the norms are paywalled and should be checked with a practising engineer.
- **Pricing hypothesis:** R$79–149/mo, or R$15–30 per report.

**B. Deterministic PTAM generator for brokers**
- **Product (one line):** a broker enters the subject property and their own comparables (manual entry or CSV, with listing URLs as evidence). The app applies NBR 14653-2 statistics: homogenization factors, outlier test, confidence interval and a fundamentação/precisão grade. It outputs a branded PTAM PDF following the COFECI 1.066/2007 structure, sold per PTAM or by monthly plan. No AI is part of the value proposition.
- **Buyer and size:** about 650k active brokers. The share that issues PTAMs is unknown. Small agencies are also buyers.
- **Pain:** PTAMs are assembled from Word models ("ptam modelo word", "ptam modelo creci"), and homogenization is required but done by hand. Fees run R$150–3,000, and PTAMs help win exclusive listings (LaudoImóvel's pitch).
- **Competitors:**
  - LaudoImóvel: R$49.97–179.90 per PTAM or R$299.90/mo; customer count not disclosed.
  - PTAM PRO: AI, hosted on netlify.app.
  - Excel spreadsheets.
  - Tools that scrape listing portals.
- **Weaknesses:**
  - AI framing.
  - Reliance on scraped portal data (terms-of-service exposure).
  - Per-report prices are high compared with remote PTAM fees of R$150–500.
- **Demand signals:** "ptam" returns 10 suggestions; "software para elaborar ptam" exists as a query.
- **Acquisition:** SEO on "ptam modelo word / como fazer ptam / ptam creci", Google Ads, Instagram and Facebook broker groups, and YouTube.
- **Build complexity:** medium (statistics module, PDF output, map and photos); about 2–3 weeks.
- **Time to first revenue:** about 2–6 weeks; impulse buying per report is likely.
- **Main risks:**
  - Legal scope: whether brokers may issue NBR 14653-based appraisals vs. engineers' exclusive competence was not verified.
  - Comparables data: avoid portal scraping; use user-supplied comparables and any open data.
  - Brokers' incomes vary widely.
  - Copycats.
- **Pricing hypothesis:** R$29–49 per PTAM or R$99/mo for 5.
- **Exclusion flags:** a terms-of-service problem if it relies on scraping; it must not be positioned as AI.

**C. Pest-control and water-tank-cleaning "service → certificate → recall" tool (vertical Jobber)**

The reminders double as rebooking.

#### Top 2 and why
1. **A, the engineering inspection report generator.** It is the only candidate that combines all of the following:
   - a proven foreign analogue (Spectora);
   - high-value buyers whose time costs about R$625/h;
   - visible template-driven search demand on six seeds;
   - fragmented, mostly rental-focused local competition with no clear leader;
   - a pure document-automation build that suits one developer working with AI coding tools.

   The value is speed and a standardized format, not AI. The free-template SEO funnel works with no contacts.
2. **B, the deterministic PTAM generator.** It has proven per-report willingness to pay (LaudoImóvel's credit packs), a very large buyer pool (about 650k brokers), strong "ptam modelo word" intent and only two or three small competitors, mostly AI-framed. Per-report pricing gives the fastest path to first revenue.
   - It ranks second because of two unverified risks: the legal scope of broker appraisals and the sourcing of comparables data.
   - If either fails due diligence, use C (pest-control certificates and recall) as the second pick. C has the same template-SEO mechanics and thin competition but weaker evidence that buyers pay.

#### Traps: popular-sounding ideas that are saturated or unworkable, with evidence
- **DJEN monitoring and deadline tools for lawyers:**
  - Incumbents are large: Astrea (R$379/5 users, 120k+ users) and ADVBOX (5k+ offices, 80k users).

- **Landlord self-management (TurboTenant clone):** at least six Brazilian apps already exist (Aluga Fácil, ProprietárioZen, Alugo, Alude, Accordous, LocaPay) at R$29.90–59.90, plus QuintoAndar.

- **Rural herd management, CAR or ITR tools:** sales are led by account managers (Aegro), the searches are dominated by free government tasks, "gestão de rebanho app" returned 0 suggestions, and distribution depends on field relationships.
